A Zero-Code, Industry-Agnostic Approach to Enterprise AI Integration

Saina Bayat | Signum Magnum College | Supervised by Prof. Dr. Walter Kurz

Research Problem

Existing Gaps in Automated AI Deployment Systems:

  • Fragmented deployment pipelines requiring manual configuration
  • Lack of semantic interoperability across domains
  • Insufficient support for dynamic adaptation
  • Absence of full-cycle automation without human intervention
  • Limited accessibility for non-technical users

Current frameworks rely heavily on manual scripting and DevOps integration, restricting scalability and accessibility in enterprise contexts (Sinha & Lee, 2024).

Research Methodology

This study employs Design Science Research (DSR) methodology (Hevner et al., 2004), focusing on:

  1. Systematic Literature Review - Zero-code platforms, API management, NLP intent recognition
  2. Expert Interviews - IT architects, compliance officers, API developers
  3. Usability Evaluation - Non-technical users test mockups with TAM-based surveys

Validation occurs through triangulation of findings from all three data sources.
